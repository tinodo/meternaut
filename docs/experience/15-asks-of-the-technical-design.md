# What the experience needs of the technical design

**What this document answers.** What the experience needs of [the technical design](../design/README.md), and where the design says it. Each row adds to one design document or changes a rule it already states; none adds a tool for an agent. The last column says what is lost without it.

## People and preferences

| Ask | The experience needs it because | Where the design says it | Without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A preferences store per person:** channel, language, working days, time zone, quiet hours, desk day, weekly budget, delegates, mutes, and Pause messages with a return date | Messages must follow what the person said, and the attention budget is counted per person | [05 Privacy, retention and recovery](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#8-privacy-retention-and-recovery) | Everyone gets the same defaults, and no budget can be per person |
| **A notification policy step in the send workflow,** with parameters: push accounting per person, slow start, one reminder (folded into the weekly desk only when the push budget is used up), coverage routing, lanes, a fatigue review | An attention budget has to be counted and kept per person | [05 Channels](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#6-channels) and [03 The Conductor](../design/03-agents-and-workflows.md#8-the-conductor) | The budget stays a promise that nothing enforces |
| **Narrow the next-person rule.** A waiting Case goes to an appointed delegate or the custodian queue, and to a line manager only when the owner appointed them; the owner is told once, by notice e-mail, when it does | Silence must not become a message about a person, or a surprise to the owner | [03 Seven workflows](../design/03-agents-and-workflows.md#4-seven-workflows), the paragraph that reads the Case diagram | A manager hears that a named person did not answer |
| **Narrow the conversation-buttons rule.** Explain, Challenge and Teach on messages that carry a claim or a decision about spend; Explain alone on the rest; "Talk to a person" as the last button of every message | Challenge and Teach mean nothing on a settings or a hello message, and help must be in the same place every time | [03 What a person receives](../design/03-agents-and-workflows.md#6-what-a-person-receives) | Utility messages carry buttons that do nothing, or the rule is broken |
| **Templates and message catalogs in Git** for cards, letters and pages, per language, with CI checks and snapshot tests | Wording is part of a message's safety and must not drift silently | [09 The repository](../design/09-delivery-and-operations.md#1-the-repository) and [08 The three tiers](../design/08-testing-and-evaluation.md#1-the-three-tiers) | Words change without review, and no comprehension check runs |
| **Web routes and their read APIs:** decisions, notes, activity and notebook; and `/ok/<requestId>`, the page of any card's request, which records nothing when opened | Owners must be able to see, correct and delete what is kept about their resources, and a client that cannot draw a card needs a page | [05 Channels](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#6-channels), web page | The promise to show what is kept cannot be kept, and a card's fallback leads nowhere |
| **"Pause changes here":** a flag an owner sets on a resource that narrows a standing permission, separate from "Fewer like this" | An owner must be able to stop automation in one place | [03 Autonomy is earned](../design/03-agents-and-workflows.md#7-autonomy-is-earned) | The only brake is the platform team's kill switch |

## Approvals and sending

| Ask | The experience needs it because | Where the design says it | Without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One decision per approver on one plan.** The design already binds the plan hash, the approver, the expiry and consumption; the ask is that the short code (eight characters from the hash) is a display reference, each approver records one decision against it, a second submit by the same person is refused, and the plan is consumed once, when it runs | Two approvers share one plan, so a code consumed by the first approval would refuse the second | [05 The approval record](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#7-the-approval-record) | The second approver is refused, or the first approver's decision can be replayed |
| **Two independent R3 approvers who are different directory objects,** asked in parallel, neither seeing the other's answer until both have decided | Independence is the point of two approvals, and one person holding both roles must not fill both | [06 Approval classes](../design/06-security-and-governance.md#approval-classes) | One person with two roles approves alone, or the second approver follows the first |
| **A daily cap that sends changes to the page.** After five one-tap approvals in a day further changes go to the page; nothing is blocked or reported. An approval recorded without the authenticated event of its route (a card delivery and tap, or a page sign-in and submit) raises an alarm and pauses that person's one-tap approvals | A stolen signed-in device must be limited without watching anyone's hours; the alarm catches forged requests, not a stolen session, and does not prove who tapped | [06 Approval classes](../design/06-security-and-governance.md#approval-classes) | A stolen device can approve without limit |
| **A rollback grant** for a change that can be reversed: usable once by the approver inside the undo window | Undoing a change should not need a second approval | [05 The approval record](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#7-the-approval-record) | Undo is slower than the change it reverses |
| **"I did not approve this":** it starts the incident path, revokes the approval if it is unused, pauses that person's one-tap approvals, alerts the platform team and keeps the record; Undo, not this button, reverses a change that ran. "Talk to a person" takes a report that a message looks wrong or fake into the same path | A person must be able to disown an approval, or report a fake, at once | [06 The approval record](../design/06-security-and-governance.md#the-approval-record) and [09 Incident playbooks](../design/09-delivery-and-operations.md#7-incident-playbooks) | A stolen or mistaken approval, or a fake message, cannot be reported where it happened |
| **A page to write, review and end a standing permission,** for the FinOps lead and the platform owner together: a dry-run on history, the evidence, the expiry and the step-down | A standing permission lets the system act without a fresh tap, so writing one needs a reviewed route and a record | [03 Autonomy is earned](../design/03-agents-and-workflows.md#7-autonomy-is-earned) and [06 Credentials, runner roles and purchases](../design/06-security-and-governance.md#credentials-runner-roles-and-purchases) | Permissions are written outside any reviewed flow |
| **A send-step rule:** strip URLs and escape markdown in every bound value; build every link from a configured origin and an opaque id (a card carries one link; a letter may carry several); fill numbers, dates and money in model-written text from tool results at render time, in the person's locale | Teams renders markdown links, and a resource name or tag may carry one | [05 Channels](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#6-channels) and [06 Where untrusted text could reach a write](../design/06-security-and-governance.md#3-where-untrusted-text-could-reach-a-write) | A persuasive resource name can become a link in a card |
| **A Teams delivery contract:** one activity per message (the notification sentence, the card, the AI-generated entity, a locale), queued per person and throttled per app and per conversation; a conversation reference stored per person and retired on a 403 blocked or uninstalled response, then e-mail; back-off on 429, 412, 502 and 504; a send of unknown result recorded as unknown and checked before a retry | Teams limits an app to 50 requests a second per tenant and a conversation to 7 messages a second, and first contact needs an installed app | [05 Channels](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#6-channels), Teams | Messages are lost, duplicated or sent to people who blocked the app |
| **A contract for every verb,** kept in the card manifest: who may tap, what a repeat does (nothing: buttons are idempotent), when a verb is refused and what a tap returns | A button that sends only an opaque id must be safe to repeat and to forward | [05 The approval record](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#7-the-approval-record) | A repeated or forwarded tap can change something twice |
| **A note-scope rule.** A note's scope never exceeds the scope its author owns; a wider scope is asked of the person who owns it, with the number of resources affected | One owner must not be able to silence another's resources | [03 What a person receives](../design/03-agents-and-workflows.md#6-what-a-person-receives) | One owner's note can widen over another's estate |
| **Cards only to members of the directory,** through the stored one-to-one conversation with the app; partners and other outsiders get e-mail only | A card that changes something must arrive only by a trusted route | [05 Channels](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#6-channels), Teams | A card could reach an outsider or arrive by an untrusted route |
| **Three rules for the external e-mail path the design already has:** an external flag, minimum estate data, no figures in the subject line | Requests to partners and billing owners must be safe to send | [05 Channels](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#6-channels), e-mail, and [14 Four purchase paths](../design/14-agreements-and-access.md#four-purchase-paths) | A request to a partner can carry estate data it does not need |
| **Where the machine-readable AI marks travel:** the AI-generated entity on a Teams message and the `x-ai-generated` header on e-mail, with a test that they survive | Card metadata needs schema 1.6, and the cards use 1.5 | [05 Channels](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#6-channels) and [06 Compliance mapping](../design/06-security-and-governance.md#11-compliance-mapping) | The marking duty relies on a mark that may not arrive |
| **Atlas proposals come from a fork.** The `pr-draft` App holds Pull requests and Checks write and no Contents write on the base repository | Merging needs Contents write, so an App that holds it can merge once the rules pass | [05 Tools](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#4-tools) | The App can merge a change that a person approved |

## Reports and money

| Ask | The experience needs it because | Where the design says it | Without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Report events for the dashboards:** small business events with no object id, counts over fewer than five people rolled up and results that a subtraction could isolate suppressed, a table retention of at least 13 months, and the workbooks kept as code | Dashboards must not expose people, and a year-on-year commitment comparison needs 13 months | [05 Where data lives](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#1-where-data-lives) and [10 What the numbers say](../design/10-cost-and-finops.md#what-the-numbers-say) | No dashboards, or dashboards that point at people |
| **A saving rule:** subtract the unused commitment a change creates from a claimed saving; hold back from Verified a reduction made while a spending commitment is behind, labelled freed capacity, until the shortfall and its prepayment credit are settled; carry the commitment's pace (as of the last invoice date, with milestones and a shortfall range, or "Cannot assess") as a report field where it is readable | A change can move cost into an unused commitment instead of removing it, and a shortfall is charged at the end date as prepayment credit | [08 Measures and the report card](../design/08-testing-and-evaluation.md#4-measures-and-the-report-card) | Such a change is counted as a saving |
| **The monthly letter and its CSV,** sent after the period closes (starting value five days after it ends), with the same figures in a small CSV and an invoice status per scope: Reconciled, Unreconciled or Invoice not available | Finance reconciles to the invoice where one is readable, and charges can change for days after a period ends | [03 Seven workflows](../design/03-agents-and-workflows.md#4-seven-workflows), the monthly timer | Finance reads estimates it cannot reconcile |
| **Feedback capture** into the ledger: "useful" and "matches what I expected" | A usefulness signal that never changes a Case | [08 Measures and the report card](../design/08-testing-and-evaluation.md#4-measures-and-the-report-card) | Nobody can tell whether messages help |

## Onboarding and operations

| Ask | The experience needs it because | Where the design says it | Without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A first-contact flow:** self-install or e-mail, an opt-out record, and an announcement that names the app, its publisher and the address of the approval page | People must be able to tell the app from a phishing message | [05 Channels](../design/05-data-and-integrations.md#6-channels), Teams, and [09 Onboarding an estate](../design/09-delivery-and-operations.md#9-onboarding-an-estate) | The first message looks like the fraud it warns about |
| **An approver readiness check:** each approver signs in once on the approval page at the strength their class needs; where one cannot pass, R3 purchases are refused with the reason | A purchase must not fail, or be weakened, at the last minute | [09 Onboarding an estate](../design/09-delivery-and-operations.md#9-onboarding-an-estate) and [06 Approval classes](../design/06-security-and-governance.md#approval-classes) | An approver finds out at the moment of approval |
| **The kill switch page and its runbook fallback:** three levels, re-authentication and an audit entry | One person must be able to stop things quickly, even when the page is down | [06 Kill switches](../design/06-security-and-governance.md#kill-switches) and [09 Incident playbooks](../design/09-delivery-and-operations.md#7-incident-playbooks) | The page that matters most in an incident has no design |
| **Later:** the voice and meeting modules, and developer cost comments | Optional modules that follow once the written flows are trusted | [13 Phases](../design/13-roadmap.md#1-phases) | Nothing is lost in the written flows |
