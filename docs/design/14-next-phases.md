# Design 14 — Next phases

> **What this answers:** *What do we prove first, in what order, and which assumptions are checked by building the smallest thing that can show them wrong?* The design of phase 1 ends with this plan; the build begins with its first spikes. Part of the [design](README.md). Decisions the plan tests are in [13](13-decisions.md); costs in [12](12-cost-and-finops.md).

---

## 1. Principle: evidence before breadth

The riskiest parts of this design are not the agents. They are the **platform seams**: whether an agent identity passes the hub's per-tool checks, whether three parties can each verify one signed approval while a constrained role-assignment delegation behaves as described, whether a Durable Functions spine survives a restart without a lost or double effect, whether a private Foundry spoke really fills its own stores, whether a Teams card can carry a bound approval. Each is a small experiment. **Every phase starts with the spikes that could invalidate its decisions and ends with evidence against a named exit criterion** — evidence the pipeline produces, not a status report.

## 4. Phases

Sizes are relative effort (**S**, **M**, **L**), not calendar promises. Each phase *proves* a claim from the north star.

| Phase | Builds | Spikes first | Proves | Exit criteria (evidence) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**What a phase never does:** widen the blast radius before the evidence exists. Phase 3 acts on one estate, three Moves, reversible only; phase 5 is the first time money moves. Until the minimum crew exists the platform runs in **single-operator mode**: promotion stops at shadow and reversible Moves. Every phase exit also reports the *measured* cost per case (per call in phase 0) next to the cost model's figure ([12](12-cost-and-finops.md)); S-COST-1 replaces the seeds as soon as its first hundred cases have run.

**Environments are created in the phase that needs them.** Dev in phase 0, the production spoke in phase 2 (shadow), the test account in phase 3 (the first release that can act needs a rehearsal), the Lab account in phase 4; a deferred private account saves its idle meters ([12 §4](12-cost-and-finops.md#4-what-still-costs-money-when-nothing-is-happening)). The one exception is the AI Search service, created on day zero because its region may refuse it (assumption 8): a deliberate idle meter that buys certainty. Two spikes wait for a trigger instead of a phase: S-GW-2 (the first A2A asset) and S-SEC-3 (egress *enforce* mode reaching general availability).

## 5. Pre-flight checklist (day zero)

1. **Quota requests** for the GPT-6 family and the hosted-agent session limits in Sweden Central.
2. **AI Search**: create the Search services on day zero, before anything depends on them. Learn flags Sweden Central as "in high demand, which prevents the creation of new search services" (assumption 8); if it refuses, create the service in another EU region and cost the cross-region traffic.
3. **Resource providers** registered in the new subscriptions (the research lists about 25 for the hub and the spoke).
4. **Entra**: the hub's app registration with the `Model.Invoke`, `MCP.Invoke`, `Agent.Invoke` roles, and the per-tool app roles to be assigned directly to the agent identities (S-GW-3); a person able to assign app roles.
5. **Networking**: address space (a `/24` per agent subnet, a `/26` for endpoints, and a separate subnet for the execution plane, `snet-exec`, sized in [04 §6](04-landing-zone-and-citadel.md#6-network)), peering both ways, firewall rules for the Entra hosts, `*.identity.azure.net`, Application Insights and the hub; private DNS zones linked.
6. **GitHub**: the repository's protected environments and rulesets, workload identity federation, the self-hosted runner job; the plan checked for required reviewers and status checks on a private repository (S-DEL-2); bot pull-request workflows on GitHub-hosted runners with no secrets and no federation.
7. **Teams and mail**: custom app allowed for pilot users; shared mailbox; Exchange role-based access scoping for the application.
8. **Billing**: Cost Management export destination; reader access on the first estate.
9. **Policy**: the initiative in *audit* mode in the first week, then *deny* in production.
10. **Budgets** as alarms on every subscription; the cost report workflow.
