## Findings

1. **Major · D\05-data-and-integrations.md:211 · "a verb is refused when its request is expired, changed, answered or paused" ·** This blanket rule also covers Undo, disowning an approval and help. It conflicts with `D\06-security-and-governance.md:141` and `E\08-attention-and-trust.md:126–127`, which keep the incident path available whatever the record shows. · **Fix:** specify allowed states per verb. Keep incident reports and help reachable during pauses and after a decision. Validate Undo against its own grant and window.

2. **Major · D\05-data-and-integrations.md:199 · "The approver who holds the rollback grant" ·** A Credential run has no individual approval, yet its resource owner must be able to Undo it (`D\03-agents-and-workflows.md:190,198`; `E\06-web-pages.md:91`). The grant text also never sets the promised R1 24-hour default (`E\08-attention-and-trust.md:46`). · **Fix:** name the holder for each execution route, including the owner for a Credential run. Bind the reverse plan, single consumption and recipe window; set the R1 starting window.

3. **Major · D\05-data-and-integrations.md:128 · "a person merges" ·** The fork rewrite removes the explicit base-branch ruleset requirement. `D\09-delivery-and-operations.md:21` names rulesets as files but supplies no replacement policy. `E\09-reviewers-and-contributors.md:91–92` requires independent code-owner review, stale-approval dismissal, no App bypass and a trusted check source. · **Fix:** retain those enforced merge conditions beside the fork path. A human merge alone is not the promised review gate. [Learn confirms required reviews are configured controls](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/maintain-secure-repository-github/2-how-to-maintain-secure-repository); [GitHub details check-source binding](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/managing-protected-branches/about-protected-branches#require-status-checks-before-merging).

4. **Major · D\05-data-and-integrations.md:170 · "a push over budget, or in quiet hours, waits" ·** This includes the weekly desk and unprompted safety notices. `E\08-attention-and-trust.md:37–43` excludes them and makes zero budget mean desk-only. `D\03-agents-and-workflows.md:194` promises run notices within minutes. · **Fix:** encode the experience's counting categories and safety exceptions. Test zero budget, an exhausted budget and a Credential run in quiet hours.

5. **Major · D\05-data-and-integrations.md:58 · "an approval record is never changed after the Approval service writes it" ·** Section 7 writes one record per request, including pending status, successive approver decisions and revocation (`188–207`). No permitted lifecycle reconciles those writes with immutability. · **Fix:** distinguish pending request state from sealed approval evidence, or specify append-only decision/revocation records. Define concurrent second-approval and revocation handling without allowing a plan to run twice.

6. **Major · D\08-testing-and-evaluation.md:137 · "a card carrying more than the opaque id" ·** The negative test contradicts required request id, verb and plan code in `D\05-data-and-integrations.md:211` and `E\04-messages-and-cards.md:210`. The old restriction also remains in `D\05-data-and-integrations.md:192` and `D\06-security-and-governance.md:129`. · **Fix:** allow exactly the manifest's fields. Reject embedded plans and undeclared fields, not every field beyond the id.

7. **Major · D\05-data-and-integrations.md:176 · "the item goes by e-mail" ·** This describes failure fallback, but no technical send rule requires an approval-waiting e-mail after successful card delivery. `E\05-letters-and-requests.md:31` and `E\06-web-pages.md:25` require it for every purchase and affected one-person clients. · **Fix:** schedule the paired e-mail explicitly, with the same request/code, expiry and network instructions. Include it in the device gate. [OpenUrl may use an embedded browser](https://learn.microsoft.com/adaptive-cards/schema-explorer/action-open-url); successful delivery is not proof of a usable approval link.

8. **Major · D\05-data-and-integrations.md:89 · "compared with the invoice's pre-tax amount" ·** A raw comparison can mark a valid month unreconciled because support charges are absent from Cost Management. `D\14-agreements-and-access.md:47` admits the exclusions; `E\07-reports-and-dashboards.md:70` and `E\05-letters-and-requests.md:64` require an explained bridge. · **Fix:** reconcile matched scope/currency totals after accounting for exclusions, not rounding alone. Preserve the explained difference. [Learn lists support, taxes, credits and offer-specific purchase exclusions](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/costs/understand-cost-mgt-data#costs-included-in-cost-management).

9. **Major · D\01-goals-and-scope.md:72 · "every message offers three buttons" ·** F5 contradicts `D\03-agents-and-workflows.md:169` and `E\04-messages-and-cards.md:46`. A builder following the requirements adds meaningless Challenge/Teach controls to Hello, Questions and settings. · **Fix:** narrow F5 to spend claims and decisions; state Explain-only for the other messages and the common help control.

10. **Major · D\13-roadmap.md:27 · "approver readiness" ·** Readiness first appears in phase 4, although phase 3 already enables R2 and `D\09-delivery-and-operations.md:202,210` requires the check during onboarding. Similarly, phase 1a sends mail through a mandatory notification step (`D\05-data-and-integrations.md:166,170`), but preferences, first contact and that policy first appear in phase 2 (`25`). · **Fix:** move each dependency to its first use: the FinOps recipient's minimal contact/preferences/policy in 1a, R2 readiness before phase 3, and R3 readiness before purchases.

11. **Major · D\04-knowledge-and-learning.md:307 · "Two Lessons from your team" ·** This is displayed Monday-digest copy, followed by "Detector retired" (`309`). It breaks `AGENTS.md` rule 13(b), not a stylistic preference: `E\README.md:30–45` reserves internal names for design text. A builder copying it ships words people are not meant to see. · **Fix:** use the established screen words in the digest and explain the practical change. Keep the internal names in the surrounding technical explanation.
