# Build plan v2, final check: disposition

The closure checker read the fixes again ([final-1.md](final-1.md)): all 15 items that remained after the closure round are closed (A7, B7, B10, B13 and N1 to N11), and the verdict is Release: YES. It found 8 new defects, 5 Minor and 3 Nit, none blocking, and said which to do first when words run short. It read the Microsoft statements that are new or changed (when a billing period closes, the lag and coverage of tags in cost data, billing period and invoice in the Cost Details API) on Microsoft Learn and found them supported; the one nuance is F2. This record says what was done with each.

| # | Finding (severity) | Verdict | What was done (where) |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | The second reader is not asked whether the price is credible, and the counting rule is worded several ways (Minor) | Accepted | The second reader agrees that the facts and the price are right ([02](../../build-plan/02-before-you-start.md#what-is-recorded-for-each-finding)); the other wordings are summaries of that rule |
| F2 | Both Learn statements are about a billing period, which varies by subscription type, so the sixth day after a calendar month does not always fit (Minor) | Accepted | "A billing period counts as closed from the sixth day after it ends", which is the calendar month under an Enterprise Agreement; for another cycle the person names the month that matches the period's end ([02](../../build-plan/02-before-you-start.md#choosing-the-first-estate), the docstring of the probe) |
| F3 | The pass allows dates only, while 02 allows a billing period or an invoice, and a date-range report is organised by the date the billing system received the cost (Minor) | Accepted | The pass says "the same period", and the fallback of C13 is to compare by billing period or invoice where 02 allows it before another estate is chosen ([03](../../build-plan/03-first-checks.md#c13-the-first-estate-offers-a-per-resource-bill-path)) |
| F4 | A cap cannot be compared with tokens, and the period of the cap is not said (Minor) | Accepted in part | Tokens are charged at their token price ([04](../../build-plan/04-cost-effort-and-risks.md#the-three-reviews)). Whether a cap is a total or a monthly figure stays a parameter of the stage, and `evaluation/stages.json` says which of the two each stage uses |

| F7 | Four elliptical wordings (Nit) | Accepted | "Three end in the reviews below", "resource types", "Compare the lines' total with Cost Management's", "a closed month's bill" |
| F8 | The changelog words the count rule a fifth way, calls the learning review's figure "at most", and lists five items not adopted where the records list seven (Nit) | Accepted | All three corrected in the changelog |
