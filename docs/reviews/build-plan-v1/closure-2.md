## New defects

**Six new defects: 0 Blocker, 0 Major, 6 Minor.** Two defects found earlier in this pass (C11 missing from the 1a.6b and 1a.7 check cells; the GitHub licence row accepting Team) and the missing same-tenant clause were fixed in the files before this report was written, and are not listed.

### N1: Minor: the chain's months drop the four-days-in-five assumption

- **Current file:line:** `01:196`; `04:65`.
- **Quote:** "about 10 to 14 months with one builder on each slice".
- **Why wrong:** The same paragraph, and `README.md:43`, assume "each builder spending four days in five". At 0.8 of a week, 43 to 59 person-weeks is 53.8 to 73.8 weeks, which is 12.4 to 17.0 months at 4.345 weeks a month. "10 to 14" is 43 and 59 weeks divided by 4.345, with no overhead. The eight-to-eleven and five-to-eight figures do apply 0.8 (56 and 76 over 1.6 give 8.1 and 10.9; over 2.4 give 5.4 and 7.3). The sentence that warns about the unshared case understates it by two to three months.
- **Fix:** "about 12 to 17 months" in both places (same word count), or say that the chain months assume full-time work.

### N2: Minor: the kill switch page is placed in the wrong phase

- **Current file:line:** `02:85`.
- **Quote:** "(by the runbook until the page exists in phase 2)".
- **Why wrong:** The roadmap lists "the kill switch page and its runbook fallback" among what phase 3 needs (`docs\design\13-roadmap.md:26`), not among phase 2's (13:25). The design describes the page and its runbook fallback without a phase (`docs\design\06-security-and-governance.md:176`, `docs\design\09-delivery-and-operations.md:155`).
- **Fix:** "until the page exists in phase 3", or drop the phase.

### N3: Minor: the Deployment Stack sentence contradicts the delivery rules, and "during creation only" is too narrow

- **Current file:line:** `02:73`.
- **Quote:** "the platform team excludes the creator's group from its deny setting for those minutes"; "during creation only".
- **Why wrong:** `docs\design\09-delivery-and-operations.md:104` gives the stack "Two excluded groups (deploy identities, platform break-glass)" that CI counts, with changes arriving "as pull requests on `infra/`", and 02:97 says nothing reaches prod except from a tag with a release record. An exclusion added for a few minutes is therefore a reviewed `infra/` change released twice (add, remove) or an out-of-band edit the design forbids. 02:73 also does not say who gives the creator a role on a production account (02:13: anyone but the pipeline needs a time-boxed elevation). Learn says the account privileges are required "during export creation or modification", not creation only (Verification 5).
- **Fix:** Say that the creator joins the existing break-glass group for the session (a membership change, not a stack change) or that the exclusion is an `infra/` change released like any other; say that the platform team grants the creator's role by time-boxed elevation; write "creation or a later change of the export".

### N4: Minor: the cost figures are Sweden Central prices, and the plan now steers organisations away from it without saying so

No text says the figures are Sweden Central prices or that another region needs the snapshot fetched again (`tools\fetch-prices.py`).
- **Fix:** One clause in 04:7: "priced for Sweden Central; in another region fetch the price snapshot again before slice 1a.1 compares fixed lines".
