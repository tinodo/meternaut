# Build plan v1, cold readers: disposition

Five readers on five model families read the written plan cold ([brief-cold-reader.md](brief-cold-reader.md)): reader 1 on order and dependencies (gpt-6.1-sol, [cold-1](cold-1.md)), reader 2 on the requests, the cost chapter and the README (gemini-4-argon, [cold-2](cold-2.md)), reader 3 on whether the checks can be passed, are honest and are safe (grok-4.7, [cold-3](cold-3.md)), reader 4 on consistency with the design and the experience design (gpt-6-sol, [cold-4](cold-4.md)) and reader 5 as a novice at the front door (claude-haiku-5.5, [cold-5](cold-5.md)). Their verdicts: "mostly", "mostly, with four blockers", "no", "no" and "mostly no". Together they filed 84 findings: 14 Blockers, 55 Majors and 15 Minors. A finding is written R3#12 for finding 12 of reader 3.

Every finding was checked against the files, and every claim about a Microsoft product was read again on Microsoft Learn on 2026-11-02 before anything changed. Where readers disagreed, the primary source decided. The earlier [round-1 record](disposition.md) says the plan adds no transport rule for mail; that was true then. The design now has a mail-flow rule that rejects mail from the shared mailbox to any address off the allow-list (design 05 §6, 09 §9), because the experience design promises it, and check C4 tests both the send step and the rule.

## What the orchestrator verified

- **AI Search regions.** Learn marks Sweden Central, West Europe, North Europe and Germany West Central "in high demand, which prevents the creation of new search services" ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/search/search-region-support)). France Central, Poland Central, Switzerland North and UK South carry no such note and are on the hosted-agent list too.
- **Subnets.** The agent subnet "can't be shared by multiple Foundry resources", and the private-endpoint subnet must be another ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/agents/how-to/virtual-networks)). A Flex Consumption app cannot share a subnet with a Container Apps environment ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/azure-functions/flex-consumption-how-to#configure-virtual-network-integration)).
- **Exports behind a firewall.** Owner or a custom role with `Microsoft.Authorization/roleAssignments/write` and `Microsoft.Authorization/permissions/read`, trusted Azure services allowed, the copy scope From any storage account, and the same tenant ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/costs/tutorial-improved-exports)).
- **The cost model's four figures outside the price API** (`tools\cost-model.py`): only the Azure Language rate is a vendor unit price, and it is marked unverified; the others are an estimate of Durable storage and two for a hub of the organisation's own.
- **GitHub.** Rulesets need GitHub Team or Enterprise ([GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/managing-rulesets/about-rulesets)), and environments in a private repository need Team or Pro ([GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/deploy/configure-and-manage-deployments/manage-environments)); a required reviewer on an environment in a private repository needs Enterprise ([GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/deployments-and-environments#required-reviewers)), which the closure check found.
- **The passkey page** says embedded WebViews cannot support passkeys and security keys issued by an external identity provider; it says nothing of Teams' own views ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/entra/identity/authentication/concept-passkey-browser-authentication-federated-identity-provider)).

- **R5 fact checks 3 and 5.** The 48 hours and the 30 minutes to 2 hours are on the pages cited; readers 1, 2 and 4 verified the first, readers 2, 3 and 4 the second, and reader 5 read only the first characters of each page.
- **R5#2, a check for slice 0.1;** **R1#2, a dependency from the lake to Atlas;** **R3#16, moving the sign-in test;** **R5#21, the label "Surprise:"**, which stays.
- **R2#15.** Design 09 already lists Cost Management Contributor among the export roles.
- **R2's deletions** of "It is a plan only" and of the scope-creep risk: both say something no other line says.
