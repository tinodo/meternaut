# FinOps Practitioner, Finance, Procurement & Agreement Review (Review 1-C)

## 1. Verdict

**Conditional pass with 2 blockers and 8 major findings.** The experience design avoids FinOps vanity metrics by separating price basis from confidence (`Scenario / Expected / Verified`), mapping to FinOps Framework 2026 personas (X27), and surfacing estimation error alongside system cost. However, two flaws break financial and agreement trust: **Example E4 (and `ok-on-page.json` / `experience-access-variants.svg`) asks two approvers to sign an irreversible €205,860 commitment at blind list prices without a quote**, and **Access Variants D (CSP) and E (Pay-as-you-go) misstate Azure billing and commitment rules** (MOSA vs. Web-Direct MCA, CSP customer self-service permissions, and €0 reservation rows in CSP customer views). Finance controllers also need an Actual-vs-Amortised toggle, MACC burn-down guardrails, and an Excel/CSV or Power BI close export beyond Workbooks (X24).

## 2. Top 5 changes I would make to the decisions

1. **Change X8 & Section 5: Split "price basis" from "cost metric (Actual vs. Amortised)" and fix Variants D & E.**
   - *Why:* A reservation-covered VM shows `$0` under Actual cost and `$X/day` under Amortised cost ([View amortized benefit costs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/reservations/view-amortized-costs)). Under CSP (Variant D), customer Cost Management shows `$0` for actual and amortised reservation usage ([Cost Management for partners](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/costs/get-started-partners)). Under Pay-as-you-go (Variant E), legacy MOSA (`MS-AZR-0003P`) cannot buy savings plans or view amortised reservation costs, whereas Web-Direct MCA (`MS-AZR-0017G`) can do both ([Billing accounts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/view-all-accounts), [Savings plan permissions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/savings-plan/permission-buy-savings-plan)).
   - *Cost:* Two profile fields (`billingAccountType`, `costMetric`) and 40 words in `01-people.md` and `04-messages-and-cards.md`.

2. **Change Section 6 (J10), Section 12 (E4), and `ok-on-page.json`: Never run R3 approval on a Variant C/D list-price Scenario.**
   - *Why:* `ok-on-page.json` and E4 ask two approvers to sign a €205,860 savings plan while displaying `"At list prices. I cannot see your rates"` and `"Your quote decides"`. No finance controller signs €206k on blind list prices—especially when an Azure Consumption Discount (ACD) or reservation alters the needed commitment ([Discount application](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/savings-plan/discount-application)). Step 1 must be **"Request a quote"**, followed by R3 approval on the returned quote.
   - *Cost:* Updating E4, `ok-on-page.json`, `experience-access-variants.svg`, and J10.

3. **Change X23 (Finance vocabulary & Verified saving): Require a four-column close bridge (`Claimed -> Baseline -> Verified EffectiveCost Delta -> Created Vacancy / MACC Impact`).**
   - *Why:* Finance controllers do not book counterfactual "comparison group" estimates (`result.json` / Monday letter) into the general ledger. Stopping a covered VM drops its `EffectiveCost` but shifts unused commitment into `UnusedReservation` / `UnusedSavingsPlan` (vacancy) on the billing subscription, yielding zero net invoice reduction unless re-utilised or exchanged.
   - *Cost:* One "Commitment & MACC interaction" line in the Verifier, monthly letter (05), and Savings ledger (07).

4. **Change X24 (Workbooks for central roles): Attach a CSV/XLSX table to the monthly finance letter and elevate the FinOps Toolkit Power BI companion.**
   - *Why:* Finance and executives live in Excel, Power BI, and e-mail, not Azure Monitor Workbooks. Workbooks require Azure RBAC (`Monitoring Reader`), lack PDF export, and have no scheduled email delivery ([Manage workbooks](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-monitor/visualize/workbooks-manage)).
   - *Cost:* One FOCUS-aligned CSV/XLSX attachment on the monthly letter (05) and 3 lines in `07-reports-and-dashboards.md`.

5. **Change Section 6 (J10) & Section 12 (E4): Surface the 1 Feb 2027 reservation exchange cutoff, $50k rolling refund cap, trade-in rules, hourly use-it-or-lose-it mechanics, and scope waterfall.**
   - *Why:* Starting **1 February 2027**, reservations purchased for services covered by savings plans (VMs, App Service, SQL DB) **are no longer exchangeable**, while earlier reservations get one final exchange or trade-in to a savings plan ([Exchanges and refunds](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/reservations/exchange-and-refund-azure-reservations), [Trade-in](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/savings-plan/reservation-trade-in)). Approvers also need the scope waterfall (RG -> Sub -> MG -> Shared: [Savings plan scopes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/savings-plan/scope-savings-plan)).
   - *Cost:* ~150 words in `03-organisation-journeys.md` (J10) and `06-web-pages.md`.

## 4. Decisions I agree with, and the strongest reason each might still be wrong

1. **X8 (Mandatory price-basis chip and saving word):** Essential for FinOps honesty; *wrong if* it omits `Amortised` vs. `Actual/Billed`, causing portal mismatches.
2. **X16 (Plain-text e-mail requests to billing owners/partners):** Matches procurement; *wrong if* it omits SKU ID, scope, billing frequency, and subscription GUID.
3. **X20 (Independent second approver for R3):** Strong audit control; *wrong if* hiding the first approver's business justification forces Finance to ping the Budget Holder before approving €205,860.
4. **X23 (FinOps Foundation terms paired with `Scenario / Expected / Verified`):** Clean bridge; *wrong if* "Verified" ignores created reservation/savings-plan vacancy.
5. **X27 (FinOps Framework 2026 personas; drop deprecated CAF roles page):** Accurate (retires 30 Nov 2026); *wrong if* it drops the link to CAF Landing Zone teams.
6. **X4 & X25 (R2/R3 approvals only on browser page with step-up auth):** Prevents spoofing and webview passkey bugs; *wrong if* mobile approvers lack passkeys enrolled on their phones.

## 5. What is missing entirely

- **Segment tailoring:** **Enterprises** need MACC burn-down, fiscal calendars, Tag Inheritance, and `x_CostCenter` chargeback exports (and no execs on the Monday letter). **Mid-size Pay-as-you-go firms** have one person wearing Platform + FinOps + Billing hats and need one combined dashboard. **CSP partners** need Partner Earned Credit (`x_PartnerCreditRate`) visibility, customer markup injection, and Partner Center cart export.
- **Invoice close & rerating state:** Distinguishing open-month estimates (rerated up to day +5 after period end) from closed invoices (`InvoiceId` populated).
- **Reservation trade-in & 1 Feb 2027 sunset journey:** Guiding owners to trade in eligible compute/DB reservations into savings plans as exchange eligibility ends.
- **Multi-currency FX split:** Separating `x_BillingExchangeRate` swings on monthly MCA commitments from usage variance.
