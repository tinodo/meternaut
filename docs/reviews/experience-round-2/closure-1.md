## New defects

- **N-1 · Blocker · `docs\experience\cards\data\your-ok.json:23` · “switched off on the last Tuesday of the month” ·** The SQL block shuts down on the very night E1 exempts for the month-end job (`docs\experience\02-owner-journeys.md:38`); the VM block keeps running. **Fix:** keep SQL auto-pause off on that Tuesday and state the exception in its To line.
- **N-2 · Major · `docs\experience\cards\data\your-ok.json:14` · “"resource": "vm-qa-web-01 to 04"” ·** Four VMs occupy one plan block despite the one-block-per-resource contract (`docs\experience\04-messages-and-cards.md:94`). **Fix:** render four individually named resource blocks from four plan rows.
- **N-3 · Major · `docs\experience\cards\data\told-afterwards.json:4` · “"requestId": "r_9vK2xQ"” ·** The E6 plan uses the same opaque request id and page URL as E1 (`cards\data\your-ok.json:4`), despite different plan codes; E2's incident reuses it too (`cards\data\our-mistake.json:4`). An id-based action could resolve the wrong request. **Fix:** give unrelated examples distinct request ids and regenerate their page URLs.
- **N-4 · Major · `docs\experience\04-messages-and-cards.md:269` · “Post in a private or shared channel” ·** The claimed incapability conflicts with `docs\experience\13-platform-register.md:28`: enabled shared-channel agents are supported ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoftteams/platform/build-apps-for-shared-private-channels)); private-channel message/card posting remains restricted. **Fix:** say this project *chooses* neither channel, and distinguish the two platform capabilities.
- **N-5 · Major · `docs\experience\01-people.md:68` · “At the rates on your bill (list prices)” ·** Variant E presents a fifth price-basis chip in this table, `04-messages-and-cards.md:197`, `07-reports-and-dashboards.md:80` and `docs\img\experience-access-variants.svg:106-107`; `docs\experience\README.md:37` defines exactly four. **Fix:** use “At the rates on your bill” as the chip everywhere; explain list pricing separately where true.
- **N-6 · Major · `docs\experience\cards\data\permission-starts.json:9` · “up to about €500 a month in expected saving per change” ·** The C17 permission cap lacks any price basis, unlike the journey's promised “at the rates on your bill” (`docs\experience\02-owner-journeys.md:126`); C8 repeats the omission (`cards\data\told-afterwards.json:10`). **Fix:** state the cap, its price basis and that it is a ceiling rather than a forecast in both messages.
- **N-7 · Major · `docs\experience\15-asks-of-the-technical-design.md:23` · “An approval recorded without a matching card delivery and tap event raises an alarm” ·** Valid approvals on a page, including card fallbacks and changes routed there after the cap, have an authenticated page submit rather than a card tap; the rule would alarm on them. **Fix:** correlate each approval with its route's authenticated event (card delivery/tap or page submit), and alarm only when that event is missing.
- **N-8 · Major · `docs\experience\14-decisions.md:30` · “"Not now", which promises a return that never comes” ·** A forbidden refusal label survives in the published decisions vocabulary, even as a rejected alternative. **Fix:** replace it with “A deferral label implying a return that was never scheduled.”
