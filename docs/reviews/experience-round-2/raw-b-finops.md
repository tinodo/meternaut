# Cold-reader review — FinOps and finance

## Verdict

**No.** A finance approver cannot safely sign the illustrated purchase: the binding currency and benefit scope are unclear, while renewal, shortfall and invoice language promises more than the stated access can establish.

## Fact checks (checked 2 November 2026)

1. **Savings-plan purchase/scope:** [Buy](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/savings-plan/buy-savings-plan), [overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/savings-plan/savings-plan-overview), [scope](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/savings-plan/scope-savings-plan): MCA/MPA priced USD; EA local currency; MCA/MPA monthly local bills can move with FX; four scope choices, not a three-subscription list. The [eligibility page](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/savings-plan/permission-buy-savings-plan) limits savings plans to EA/MCA/MPA, although the overview confusingly mentions PAYG credit-card billing. **Do not infer PAYG savings-plan eligibility**; [PAYG reservations are documented](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/reservations/prepare-buy-reservation).
2. **Reservation renewal/refunds:** [Renewal](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/reservations/reservation-renew), [refunds](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/reservations/exchange-and-refund-azure-reservations): default auto-renew on, renewal price available at 30 days, and refund exclusions apply. The generic 30-day e-mail assertion at `03:58` also fails for reservation terms under a year (five-day notice).
3. **MACC:** [Track MACC](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/benefits/macc/track-consumption-commitment): direct EA/MCA billing-role visibility is supported; missed milestones or final commitment generate shortfall **prepayment credit**, not a simple lost-money line.
4. **Costs/invoices/budgets:** [Cost Management data](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/costs/understand-cost-mgt-data), [budgets](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/costs/tutorial-acm-create-budgets): pre-invoice estimates and later rerating are real; credits/taxes/support are excluded, PAYG commitment purchases absent; budget evaluation uses **actual cost, not amortisation**. “As invoiced” is an internal view choice, not a guaranteed invoice match.
5. **Partner costs:** [Cost Management for partners](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/costs/get-started-partners): a customer with enabled access sees retail costs; the partner sees its invoiced rates; reserved usage in the customer view can show zero. Retail verification cannot certify partner-invoiced savings.

**FinOps terms:** The [FinOps Foundation glossary](https://www.finops.org/assets/terminology/) supports run-rate reduction, cost avoidance, budget-relative “savings,” amortised cost and vacancy as unused commitment; the confidence words are local to this design, not Foundation terms. [FOCUS effective savings rate](https://focus.finops.org/docs/use-cases/v1-4/determine-effective-savings-rate/) is `(Contracted Cost − Effective Cost) / Contracted Cost`; give that formula in `07:99` rather than “effective cost as a discount.”
