## The one tension, and how it was resolved

**C asked for a hard, baseline-controlled falsifier; D called gates and "honest unknowns" lists paperwork.** Both are right about different things. The falsifier is now **step zero of a first experiment**, written as a plain description of what is compared with what and what a good result looks like — no gate, no kill criterion, no register. The unknowns that were a bullet list are now the *questions the experiment answers*.
