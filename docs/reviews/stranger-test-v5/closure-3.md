## New defects

- **N-1 · Major · `docs\01-north-star.md:128` ·** “Both go into the Atlas as observations” · A single team's first Prism bill lines enter the *public* Atlas, contradicting the several-organisation, privacy-reviewed aggregate gate in `02-learning-azure.md:184,273`; `01-north-star.md:111` also lists “bills” among public-profile receipts. This breaks the estate/Commons boundary. **Fix:** “Both findings stay in the estate; only privacy-reviewed scope-and-aggregate contributions from several organisations may become public Atlas observations. Bill receipts stay in the estate.”
- **N-2 · Major · `docs\01-north-star.md:168` ·** “the evidence, the scope, the quote, the saving — for that person, and watches the bill” · The partner/central-team handoff can occur with *no* bill access (`01-north-star.md:160`, `03-estate-and-investigation.md:40`): its figure is then a list-price scenario, not a saving, and this identity cannot watch the bill. **Fix:** “Send a bill-backed saving where available, otherwise a labelled list-price scenario; if bills are unreadable, ask the purchasing party to confirm the outcome.”
- **N-3 · Major · `docs\01-north-star.md:332` ·** “The Surveyor works out what it may see and do, inventories and reads the bill.” · This unconditional first-day account promises bill reading, meter decoding and an Unexplained Spend list even for `01-north-star.md:160`'s no-cost-access subscriber; `03-estate-and-investigation.md:262` correctly starts that subscriber from resources and metrics. **Fix:** “On day one, map reach and resources; read bills and start the Unexplained Spend list only where costs are visible, otherwise offer labelled list-price scenarios.”
- **N-4 · Major · `docs\04-finding-out-without-spending.md:109` ·** “contributes **observations** — never data — to the open Atlas” · The same sentence says a scope and an aggregate leave the estate: an aggregate *is data*, and a rare scoped cohort needs the stated privacy review, not an absolute denial. **Fix:** “With consent, contribute only privacy-reviewed scope and aggregate data, never bill lines or identifiers; publish after several organisations corroborate it.”
- **N-5 · Major · `docs\01-north-star.md:367` ·** “Learning provisions nothing just to measure; the one exception is the rare trial” · Trial is an exception to *no Azure charge*, not to *no provisioning*: `01-north-star.md:243` and `04-finding-out-without-spending.md:117` say it changes something already running. **Fix:** “Learning never provisions resources just to measure; a rare owner-approved Trial changes an existing resource and can move the bill.”
