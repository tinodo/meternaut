## New defects

- **N-1 · Major · `docs\research\cost-trap-bank.md:147` · “Deleting backup items, or the vault itself, does not end the cost”** · The absolute includes default 14-day vaulted soft deletion, which has no retention charge. It contradicts the exception already stated in BAK-001 (:144) and the cited [Pricing section](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/backup/secure-by-default). A source-verified gold case must distinguish retained data from chargeable retention. **Fix:** “Deleting backup items or a vault can leave charges for retention longer than 14 days and for operational backups; default vaulted retention is free.”
- **N-2 · Major · `docs\research\cost-trap-bank.md:147` · “Low risk, just delayed”** · The fix now includes manual deletion of operational backup snapshots, not merely waiting. The same bank rates recovery-point deletion Medium at :145. [Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/backup/secure-by-default) says these snapshots “can be directly accessed and deleted before the soft-delete period expires”. The risk field no longer describes the proposed action. **Fix:** “Medium risk for manual snapshot cleanup: confirm required recovery data and retention obligations before an owner-approved deletion.”
- **N-3 · Major · `docs\research\cost-trap-bank.md:89` · “unless a tier was set explicitly”** · This is not the only exception to unique-block billing. Its cited [snapshot page](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/storage/blobs/snapshots-overview) states: “For storage accounts that leverage smart tier, versions and snapshots are billed at full content length.” [Smart-tier guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/storage/blobs/access-tiers-smart) confirms that explicitly tiered blobs do not enter smart tier. The new rule underprices protected data in that supported configuration. **Fix:** “Unique-block billing applies without explicit tier changes or smart tier; explicit tier changes follow full-content rules, and smart-tier versions and snapshots bill at full length.”
